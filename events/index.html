Transaction Innovation Forum, Tokyo

Michael Salmony is co-creator and chairman, with Harry Smorenberg, of the Transaction Innovation Forum (TIF), the B2B forum on payments, treasury and trade in Japan. The inaugural forum took place on 20th November 2023 at the Tokyo American Club, with simultaneous Japanese/English interpretation. The second annual APAC Transaction Innovation Forum followed in Tokyo on 5th September 2024 with a focus on risk and security.

Topics of the 2023 programme: operational excellence in cross-border B2B payments, cash and treasury management innovation, CBDC, ISO 20022, implementing Open Finance/Open Banking, trade finance, digital identity, security, and regulatory developments.
