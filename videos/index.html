Further talks, interviews and webinars

Asia Payments Connect (APC) HardTalks and round tables

As member of the Asia Payments Connect (APC) Global Expert Council, Michael Salmony hosted the APC HardTalks and moderated APC panels and expert round tables in 2020-21, with guests such as the CEO of Jumio, Bo Harald, SAS, Ekata and Paul Brisk, on Open Banking, digital identity, data governance, CBDC and ISO 20022 (APC site).

ETPPA “Everything FinTech” interviews

Host of the ETPPA video interview series “Everything FinTech” on YouTube, with guests from the ECB, the World Bank, EPI, the Berlin Group, Tink, Trustly, Form3 and others.

The Payments Trilogue

A bi-weekly video podcast on European payments, discussing key trends with regulators, banks and other stakeholders.
Michael Salmony moderates as the independent voice, Ralf Ohlhausen brings the fintech view and a banker the bank view: first Gijs Boudewijn, then Javier Santamaría and now Christian Schäfer.
