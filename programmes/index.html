Tokenisation of Money and Stablecoins: executive masterclass

An executive masterclass for banking, payments and central banking leaders. It is the companion masterclass to the Springer book "Tokenisation of Money: From Fiat Currencies to Stablecoins" (July 2026) and is built on modules already being delivered at the EBA Academy in Frankfurt and Paris and at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford.

Stablecoins, CBDCs and tokenised deposits are moving from pilot to mainstream, but board-level understanding is lagging the business, strategic and compliance impacts of the technology. The masterclass is the business and strategy view: what tokenised money means for revenue, risk and competitive position.

What participants learn

  • Tokenisation and the taxonomy of money tokens: crypto, stablecoins, CBDC, deposit tokens, tokenised deposits, e-money tokens; the underlying technology - what to know and what to ignore
  • Stablecoins in depth: how they really work, the stablecoin stack, wallets and use cases, hype and reality
  • Tokenised deposits and the tokenisation of securities and other assets
  • CBDC and the Digital Euro, retail and wholesale: the outlook for banks and merchants
  • What is actually happening: market sizes, ecosystems and consortia, the moves of banks, BigTech and new challengers; what has worked, what has quietly failed and what is merely announced
  • Regulation: the EU's MiCA, the US GENIUS Act and what is happening on tokenisation around the world, and what it means for you
  • New risks and compliance: supervision, KYC/AML and integrity risks
  • Strategic implications: deposit flight risk, new revenue pools, who earns what; defensive and offensive options for banks
  • Interactive workshop: what this means for your organisation in the next 12-36 months, the questions to put to management and the no-regret moves

Who should attend

Banking and payments executives, C-suite and board members; chief digital and strategy officers and heads of innovation; treasury, risk, compliance and regulatory affairs leads; central bankers, regulators and policymakers; FinTech leaders, payment service providers, acquirers and advisers.

Format

Half-day executive briefing, full-day masterclass or board session (for example Board and Executive Committee, with a separate session for senior staff), in person or online; under Chatham House rules on request, with a short follow-up note recording the conclusions and open questions.

Training materials: slides, videos, case studies, interactive sessions (breakout workshops, tests/quizzes, etc), suggested reading lists and further materials for study.

China study tour: Rethinking the Financial Ecosystem

"Learning from China's Financial Revolution": what is the secret sauce behind China's success in finance and beyond - and what can Europe learn from it? A 7-day fact-finding mission to Shenzhen, Hangzhou and Beijing for senior executives, focused on China's edge and on new perspectives for the future of financial services, organised by Michael Salmony with the renowned global event organiser Harry Smorenberg (founder of the World Pension Summit, the Global Payment Summits in EMEA, Asia and Oceania, and the International EPCA Summit) and with a leading academic in international economics in Europe and China (teaching in Shanghai, Beijing and Sichuan) with decades of experience in international relations.

  • Senior-level sessions with leading Chinese technology, finance and industrial companies, a leading university and the central bank (planned visits, to be confirmed)
  • Finance at the core: super-app payments, digital banking, the e-CNY and the world's largest banks, set within China's wider drivers: AI, robotics, speed, management style and mindset
  • China as it lives: paying for everything with Alipay or WeChat Pay from day one, the high-speed train, dinners with young entrepreneurs
  • Learning built in: personal intake, pre-departure briefing, the same strategic questions at every host, a debrief after each session and a closing session on lessons for Europe

For executives in banks, payment providers, insurers, finance and selected industrial firms; a group of 15 to 20. This programme will be introduced in early 2027. Please contact us for more details and reservations to participate.

B2B events: Transaction Innovation Forum

Michael Salmony is co-creator and chairman, with Harry Smorenberg (see above), of the Transaction Innovation Forum (TIF), the B2B forum on payments, treasury and trade. No commercial messages: a full day of plenary sessions, expert break-outs and invitation-only round tables. The inaugural forum took place on 20th November 2023 at the Tokyo American Club, with simultaneous Japanese/English interpretation. The second annual APAC Transaction Innovation Forum followed in Tokyo on 5th September 2024 with a focus on risk and security.

Typical topics: operational excellence in cross-border B2B payments, cash and treasury management innovation, CBDC, ISO 20022, implementing Open Finance and Open Banking, trade finance, digital identity, security, and regulatory developments.

Open Banking masterclass

"Open Banking: transforming banking and the financial industry" - a compact and in-depth masterclass, typically an intensive two-day course in person, tailored to the participants: what Open Banking is and why it is happening, its key elements (APIs and consent), implementation, business models and the consequences for banks and their customers, use cases, and the outlook from Open Banking to Open Finance and beyond. It has been delivered around the world, in Europe, the US, the Middle East and beyond. For senior management, product development, strategy, marketing, data services, retail and corporate services, legal and compliance, and IT in banks, regulators, FinTechs and service providers.
