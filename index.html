Regulation and technology-driven payments innovation

PAYMENTS INNOVATION CONSULTING is a specialised consultancy focusing on regulation and technology-driven payment innovations with a special focus on Payments, FinTech, Tokenised Money/Stablecoins/CBDC and Open Finance.

We work together with all stakeholders in the domain, including central banks, policy units of regulators/supervisors, transaction processing and digital transformation units of commercial banks, growth and strategy units of neo-banks, global consultancies, third parties, associations, solution providers, payment processors, card organisations, corporates and merchants. To these we offer services of Business Consultancy, Market Research and Training in the areas of innovation in payments.

Our special expertise is Payment-related innovations such as Digital Finance, Open Finance, PSD2, FinTech, e-Wallets, Digital Identity, CBDC/Digital Money, B2B/SCF/eInvoicing, Transaction Strategy and more general areas such as policy/legislation and innovation management.

Our highly bespoke services - consistently 5* rated - are by personal introduction only but if you would like more information we look forward to hearing from you.
